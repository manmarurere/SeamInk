# Changelog

## v1.0.0

First public release.

- Bake transparent PNG tattoos from a 3D body into the skin texture, continuous across UV seams.
- Load the body model and skin texture you currently use from your Penumbra settings (choose race and gender).
- Read FFXIV `.mdl` (v5/v6), `.mtrl` and `.tex` (B8G8R8A8, BC1/BC2/BC3/BC7) directly.
- Export `.tex` (uncompressed B8G8R8A8 with mipmaps), PNG, or the tattoo alone as a transparent PNG.
- Japanese / English interface.
